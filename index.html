A message of awareness and support.
The idea

Support should be within reach.

Students may hesitate to report harassment because they fear stigma, exposure, not being believed, or having to report to someone they do not trust. SafeSpace proposes a private route to guidance through a school-based local network.

◎

Private reporting

A student can submit a concern without entering a name and receive a random case code for follow-up in the proposed system.

♡

Professional support

A trained, designated counselor can provide guidance and explain available options.

⌁

Works offline

The concept uses school Wi-Fi and a local server, rather than depending on cellular data or open internet access.

Safety, dignity and learning.

SafeSpace combines a reporting and support channel with an offline study hub. The study hub is intended to make the platform useful for everyday learning as well as student support.

Explore the Study Hub

When someone needs help, listening matters.

A report should be handled carefully, respectfully and by people with the right training. Serious or urgent cases may need referral through an agreed process to the appropriate protection authorities.
