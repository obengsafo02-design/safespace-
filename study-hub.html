Offline learning

Learn, share and collaborate.

The proposed Study Hub gives students a useful everyday reason to connect to the same school-based platform.

A learning space on the local network

Alongside student support, SafeSpace includes an offline academic collaboration hub. Students could use it to form study groups, share learning materials and collaborate on assignments without relying on mobile data.

The live version would host approved resources on the school's local server and would need moderation and clear rules for safe use.
